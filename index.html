Defqon.1 Festival Website

Defqon.1 Festival is an annual music festival held in the Netherlands, Australia and Chile. It was founded in 2003 by festival organizer Q-dance. The festival plays mostly hardstyle and related genres such as hardcore techno, hard house and hard trance.
